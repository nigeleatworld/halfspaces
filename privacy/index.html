Privacy

How we handle your information

This site has no forms or sign-ups. We count visits using Cloudflare Web Analytics, without tracking cookies. The only way to reach us through the site is email.

What we get

If you email us, we get your email address, any name you give and whatever you put in your message. Please send only what we need to understand your enquiry, and if it mentions other people, such as your staff or customers, make sure you’re allowed to share it.

What we use it for

We use your enquiry to reply to you and, if we work together, to manage that work. We don’t use it for marketing, and we don’t sell it or share it with anyone except the services below, or when the law requires it.

Services that handle it

Cloudflare hosts this site and Google Fonts supplies its fonts, so when you visit, your browser connects to both and sends them technical details such as your IP address. Our email runs on Google Workspace. These services may process data outside Singapore.

Counting visits

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. When a page loads, your browser sends Cloudflare the page’s full web address, the page that linked you here (when your browser shares it), your browser and operating system details, performance details such as loading times and memory use, and a random code for that one page load. It sets no cookies and doesn’t use your browser’s storage. What we see is a dashboard of totals, such as visits, page views and countries.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to work are deleted within 12 months of our last reply. If we work together, the confidentiality agreement says how long we keep your information and when we delete it.

If you become a client

Before we start client work, we both sign a confidentiality agreement. It sets out how we handle the information for that work, including any personal data, and how long we keep it. It adds to this page; it doesn’t take away any rights you have under the law.

Your questions and requests

To see the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected, or withdraw your consent, email our Data Protection Officer, Nigel Lin, at nigel@halfspaces.co. The same address takes questions, complaints and requests for our data protection policies. If withdrawing consent means we can’t reply or carry on the work, we’ll tell you.
