Get help

Contact Nigel, Bitpybit’s developer, if something is confusing or is not working.

Email Bitpybit support

Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version and what happened. Please do not send passwords, payment card details or sensitive workplace data.

Your saved learning

Your answers and place are saved on your device. Open the same foundation session or practical case to continue. Completed practical cases can be reviewed or repeated; a repeat uses the same example with shuffled choices.

Settings includes Export progress and Reset learning progress. Export before resetting if you want to keep a copy. An export is a readable record; the app does not currently import it as a backup.

Reminders and sound

Reminders and brief answer sounds are optional. Change them in Settings. Bitpybit has no background music.

Purchases

Foundation practice and the first practical topic are free. The optional full case library is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

To recover a purchase, use the same Apple Account that bought it and choose Restore Purchases in Bitpybit Settings or on the purchase screen. Buying and restoring require an internet connection. Resetting learning progress does not remove your purchase.

If the App Store price cannot load, try again when connected. Free learning remains available. Apple manages billing and refund requests through Report a Problem.

Privacy policy

Updated 22 September 2026. This policy covers the Bitpybit iOS app.

Your learning data

Bitpybit stores settings, foundation and practical-case answers, repeat attempts, practice history, review scheduling and your current session locally on your device. No app account is required. The app does not send your learning history to the developer and contains no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics tools.

Device backups may include app data, depending on your Apple settings. Your data may therefore leave the device through backups you use.

Exports and support

You choose whether to export your progress, whether to share it, and which app or service receives it. Exported copies are controlled by you and the services you choose.

If you email support, the developer receives what you include and uses it to respond to your request. Email is handled by your chosen mail provider and the developer’s email provider. Include only information needed to describe the problem.

Notifications

Reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification server is involved. Turn them off in Bitpybit Settings or iOS Settings.

Deleting progress

Reset learning progress clears saved foundation and case answers, repeat attempts, review scheduling, streak and current session. It keeps your preferences. It does not delete previous exports, emails you sent or existing device backups. Removing the app removes its local container, subject to Apple’s device-backup and restoration behaviour.

Apple purchases

Apple handles in-app payments and provides purchase verification so Bitpybit can unlock and restore the paid case library. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Learning content is bundled on the device; buying and restoring use Apple’s services. Purchases are managed through your Apple Account.

Questions and changes

Contact nigel@halfspaces.co with privacy questions. This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change. It is also available inside Bitpybit Settings.
