01

Positioning research for Keynote Learning.

Delivered2026Client work

My first independent research client: a Singapore tuition centre that wanted to know which position it could credibly own. An online survey of 300 screened parents, a gap analysis, and a competitor scan, delivered as one recommendation the client could act on.

How I worked

Senior-direct, no agency layer. I designed the instrument, ran the analysis, and turned the findings into a clear recommendation. I used AI to accelerate production and quality checks; the research design, analysis, and interpretation were mine.

  • Importance is ranked, not rated. A forced top-3 ranking shows trade-offs instead of politeness.
  • Satisfaction is a net, not a mean. % like minus % dislike separates approval from friction.
  • The zones are relative. Median splits survive a skeptical CFO.
  • Small cells stay out. Any cut under n=30 is left out rather than dressed up.

Keynote’s findings stay with Keynote. The exhibit below is the same method run on a fictional brand, with synthetic data.

Kopi Lima, a fictional brand · importance vs. satisfaction

Speed and seating carry the widest gaps; taste is already a strength. The territory worth testing is the fast, reliable kopi run.

Base: 400 synthetic respondents. Importance = % ranking the factor top-3. Net = % like − % dislike. Dashed lines split each axis at its median. Numbers on the dots are hoverable.

02

Turning listening into searchable memory.

Livespacepod.co

I’d listen to podcasts and forget a lot of what was said. SpacePod is a podcast knowledge base: followed shows become transcripts, transcripts become searchable memory, and search becomes cited synthesis.

Where it stands

Live, publicly usable, and early; it has not yet earned commercial validation. I run it against explicit keep-or-kill criteria rather than letting it drift. Flask, Postgres with pgvector, Deepgram, Claude, Stripe.

03

Reading personality from a cai fan order.

Research alphacft.halfspaces.co

Everyone in Singapore has a cai fan ritual: the queue, the tray, the gravy call at the end. The Cai Fan Test is a behavioural instrument dressed as lunch, calibrated against an established Big Five measure. Take it if you’re curious.

Where it stands

A working research alpha, fourteen builds in, live and taking its first real respondents. The twelve plate reads are provisional, and they stay that way until the calibration pilot says otherwise. Held to research standards from day one: separate consent, deletion codes, versioned scoring, low-base suppression.
