Where the day goes

Hours often disappear into small tasks: chasing updates, finding files, typing the same details twice.

  1. 09:10

    “Which quote did we send them, version three or four?”

    Information you can find. The latest version of a quote, a contract or a photo, in one place, found in seconds.

  2. 13:45

    “Did the supplier confirm Thursday?”

    Follow-ups that don’t slip. What’s been promised, by whom, and what still needs a nudge.

  3. 17:30

    “I’m typing the same details into three places again.”

    Do it once. Details entered once and reused, so evenings aren’t spent on copying.

  4. 22:40

    “Still answering messages.”

    Hours back. The routine replies and reminders handled, with you still approving anything that matters.

Sometimes the leak is a person or a habit, not a tool. We look at that too, and say so plainly.

Typical examples; not any one client’s day.

How working with us goes

We start with questions, not a template. We don’t ask what your biggest problem is; we ask what happened on your last job, and the fix takes shape from there.

  1. A conversation

    You tell us what eats your week. It costs nothing, and you’ll know by the end whether we can help.

  2. A walkthrough of your real work

    We follow one recent job from the first message to the final payment, on your own phone and files, and mark where time and information leak.

  3. One small pilot

    We fix the one thing that costs you most, with the simplest thing that works, and measure the hours it saves. You decide everything.

  4. A handover you own

    It runs without us. It’s written down, you know how to fix it, and our support ends on a date you agree to.

What we won’t do

  • Arrive with the answer before we’ve heard the problem.
  • Sell you software you don’t need.
  • Add AI for the sake of saying we did.
  • Leave you with something only we can fix.
  • Send a message, quote or payment on your behalf without your say-so.
