“Is this the latest quote? The price is different from last week.”

Mrs Tan, a client, on WhatsApp

Four files called “final”, and ten minutes later he’s still not sure which one she saw; each time the quote changed he saved another copy, and every copy got called final.

+10 minutes. Each quote is now one link that’s always the latest, with old versions kept in its history, and changes he describes in a voice note are drafted in for him to approve.

“Do you deliver to Punggol?”

What happens after you email

The first conversation is free and takes about 45 minutes, in person or on a call. We’ll ask what’s eating your week and go through your last job with you, from the first message to the final payment, on your own phone and files. If there’s something worth fixing, we’ll tell you what we’d try first, how long it would take and what it would cost you to keep running; if there isn’t, we’ll say that too.

If you go ahead, we fix one thing at a time, time the task before and after, and hand it over written down so it runs without us. Nothing we set up sends a message, quote or payment on your behalf without your say-so, and we won’t add AI for the sake of saying we did.
