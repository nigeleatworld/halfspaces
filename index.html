01 / Client research

Positioning research for Keynote Learning.

2026/Delivered/client work

My first independent research client. Keynote Learning, a Singapore tuition centre, wanted to know which position it could credibly own in a crowded market, so I ran the study end to end and handed back a recommendation it could act on.

What it is

A positioning study for Keynote Learning, a Singapore tuition centre. An online survey of 300 screened parents, an importance versus satisfaction gap analysis, and a competitor scan, delivered as an exhibit deck, a prose insights piece, and a data table.

How I worked

Senior-direct, no agency layer. I designed the instrument, ran the analysis, and turned the findings into a clear recommendation. I used AI to accelerate production and quality checks; the research design, analysis, and interpretation were mine.

Where it stands

Delivered as three artefacts; the findings stay with the client. This is the shape of work I take on now.

01 · The brief

A mid-sized kopi chain wants a position it can own.

Kopi Lima is an invented 14-outlet chain competing against bigger names with more stores and louder marketing. Every page of this study, including the client, is made up; the way of working is the part that’s real.

03 · The choices behind it

Four decisions do the analytical work.

Importance is ranked, not rated.

Rating scales let everything matter. Forcing a top-3 ranking makes respondents spend a budget, so the chart shows trade-offs instead of politeness.

Satisfaction is a net, not a mean.

% like minus % dislike separates polite approval from real friction; a factor can average fine while still costing visits.

The zones are relative.

Median splits mean the opportunity zone reads more important and less satisfying than the rest of the set, not disliked. That framing survives a skeptical CFO.

Small cells stay out.

Any segment cut under n=30 is left out of the story rather than dressed up. The rule costs a few findings and buys trust in the rest.

04 · The recommendation

Build toward the fast, reliable kopi run; use taste as supporting proof.

1

Fix peak throughput before claiming it. A speed claim the queue contradicts is worse than no claim; earn it operationally first.

2

Stage the message. Lead with the seat promise where it already holds, extend to speed as service times close on the target.

3

Validate before spending on it. This exhibit finds the territory; a competitor read and a claim-credibility check decide whether Kopi Lima can take it.

What I’d watch
  • Peak-hour walk-aways · counted at the door, not assumed
  • Repeat visits · weekly, by outlet
  • Seat wait at peak · minutes, sampled
  • Claim recall · aided, quarterly

02 / SpacePod

Turning listening into searchable memory.

2026/Live/spacepod.co

I’d listen to podcasts and forget a lot of what was said. SpacePod helps keep the wisdom within reach.

What it is

A podcast knowledge base: followed shows become transcripts, transcripts become searchable memory, and search becomes cited synthesis.

Why I built it

03 / The Cai Fan Test

Reading personality from a cai fan order.

2026/Research alpha/cft.halfspaces.co

Everyone in Singapore has a cai fan ritual: the queue, the tray, the two-to-four dishes, the gravy call at the end. I’m building a personality assessment out of it.

What it is

A behavioural instrument dressed as lunch. A first-person stall visit records what you pick, in what order, what you swap out, and what you wished was on the counter; a calibration module ties the food behaviour to an established Big Five measure.

Why I built it

Personality tests here are imports. I wanted one grown from a ritual Singaporeans perform daily, held to research standards from day one: separate consent, deletion codes, versioned scoring, low-base suppression.

Where it stands

A working research alpha, fourteen builds in, live at cft.halfspaces.co; take it if you're curious. The twelve plate reads are provisional, and they stay that way until real respondents calibrate them; the first calibration pilot is underway.
